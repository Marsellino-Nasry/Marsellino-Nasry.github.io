## 👋 About me

I'm a **Senior IT Auditor at BDO Khaled & Co. (BDO Egypt)** with more than five years of cybersecurity experience. I have led **30+ ITGC, information security and cybersecurity audits** for organisations in banking and financial services, energy and petroleum, retail, FMCG and fintech.

My work looks at every technology environment from two sides:

- 🎯 **The adversary's view:** how an attacker would discover, exploit and escalate a weakness.
- 🛡️ **The auditor's view:** whether the controls around it are properly designed, implemented and operating effectively.

Putting the two together means my findings are grounded in how attacks actually happen, and my recommendations are written in terms of business risk.

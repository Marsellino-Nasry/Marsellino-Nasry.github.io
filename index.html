Two disciplines, one view of risk

Most security work looks at a system from one side. My work looks at it from both: how it would be attacked, and whether the controls around it would hold.

I'm a Senior IT Auditor at BDO Khaled & Co. (BDO Egypt) with more than five years of experience across banking, petroleum, and corporate environments. My work sits where technical security meets governance: I assess whether technology controls are designed well and operate as intended, and I test whether they stand up to a real attacker.

On the assurance side, I deliver IT General Controls audits, IT risk assessments, Test of Design and Test of Operating Effectiveness procedures, and Risk and Control Matrix development. I've assessed identity and privileged access, segregation of duties, change management, IT operations, business continuity, and vulnerability management across core banking, ERP, and directory environments, aligned with ISO/IEC 27001, COBIT 2019, COSO, NIST, and PCI DSS.

On the offensive side, I practise web, network, and Active Directory penetration testing, red team techniques, and responsible vulnerability research through bug bounty programmes. That hands-on work, which places me in the top 1% of TryHackMe users worldwide, shapes how I audit: findings are grounded in how weaknesses are actually exploited, and recommendations are ranked by business impact.

I also build tools. My ISO/IEC 27001:2022 ISMS Assurance Platform supports audit and certification-readiness work end to end, and the SARM Security Arsenal automates reconnaissance, vulnerability analysis, and CVE intelligence for my own assessments.

How I work

The three arrows in the SARM mark stand for these three stages.

  1. Assess

    Understand the business, scope the environment, and evaluate control design against ISO/IEC 27001, COBIT, and COSO. The result is a gap view ranked by risk.

  2. Validate

    Test how controls perform in practice: operating-effectiveness sampling, vulnerability assessment, and penetration testing that shows what an adversary could actually reach.

  3. Assure

    Report findings in business terms, agree remediation with control owners, and confirm that fixes hold. Management gets the risk picture; technical teams get what they need to act.

Projects

Tools I've built to make audit and security work more rigorous and repeatable.

ISMS Assurance Platform for ISO/IEC 27001:2022

A browser-based platform that brings ISO/IEC 27001:2022 audit, risk, and compliance work into one place. I built it to support Lead Auditor activities and to help organizations implement an ISMS and prepare for certification readiness.

  • Clauses 4 to 10 conformity assessment, with evidence and rationale recorded per requirement
  • Annex A assessment of all 93 controls, with ISO/IEC 27002:2022 attributes
  • Risk register with 5×5 likelihood and impact scoring, residual risk, and heat map
  • Statement of Applicability with justification and implementation status
  • Findings, nonconformities, and corrective action tracking
  • Readiness and maturity indicators for compliance monitoring and management review
  • Audit report and SoA export to PDF and Excel
  • Local-first: working data stays in the browser, with JSON backup and restore
Built with
HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Chart.js, SheetJS, jsPDF
Status
Version 2.0, open source under the MIT licence

SARM Security Arsenal

My in-house toolkit for the reconnaissance, vulnerability analysis, and threat intelligence stages of an assessment. Used only against systems I'm authorized to test.

Built with
Python, Bash, Nmap and Nuclei output, NVD and CISA KEV data
Status
Private toolkit; source not publicly released

  1. VAMPIRE-X

    Vulnerability analysis and management

    Correlates Nmap and Nuclei results, cross-references the CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, and produces a remediation plan ranked by risk.

    $ ./vampirex.py 10.10.1.0/24 -s vuln

  2. SARM-Security

    Reconnaissance and attack surface mapping

    Discovers subdomains and technologies, flags exposed data, and builds a map of the external attack surface.

    $ ./sarm-security -d example.com

  3. SARM-CVE-DB

    CVE and threat intelligence

    Aggregates NVD and CISA feeds into one searchable hub for fast lookups and quicker risk assessment.

    $ ./sarm-cvedb --query Log4j

The Dual-Lens method: the same weakness, seen twice

Adversary lensA former employee's admin account still signs in.
Assurance lensDo leaver controls and privileged access reviews remove it? (ISO A.5.18, A.8.2)
A standard user reaches Domain Admin via a weak service account.
Are service accounts managed, least-privileged and monitored? (ISO A.8.2, A.8.16)
An exposed service has a known exploited vulnerability.
Does vulnerability management cover it and fix it within SLA? (ISO A.8.8)
